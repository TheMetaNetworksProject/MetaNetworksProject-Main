Here are some notes about the observations I recorded! As you go through, make sure to check the source and excerpt of each observation. 

**Row 2**
- The habitat section mentions they like to inhabit cacao plantations! Cacao is a specific species, and the passage indicates the trees provide habitat to the birds, which is the inquillinism interaction.

**Row 3**
- The diet page mentions the birds like insects. This is a specific Class. It also mentions fruit, but that could mean a variety of different plants and we don't go higher than Class-level taxonomy.

**Row 4**
- Following monkeys is a perfect example of commensalism! The monkeys are not harmed but the birds benefit. However, the text mentions (possibly), so we will indicate this as a weak interaction.

**Rows 5-11**
- These rows are all different pieces of dietary information obtained from the text! Remember, each interaction gets a separate row but it helps to copy repeating information (like the excerpt) down rows. Also, make sure to check the pictures for potentially identified food items!

**Row 12**
- Chasing another bird is classic competition, but since we don't know what it was over (food, territory) we will leave is as base competition.

**Rows 13-22**
- These are all from the Dickens source, which notes some dietary information about Trogons! Make sure you get the taxonomic resolution correct -- this can always be checked on GBIF or Wikipedia.
